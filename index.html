what it is

ph_01
listens
analyzes any audio, one slice at a time · stereo ins are summed to mono
redraws
up to 33 oscillators rebuild that spectrum · the output is mono
remembers
freeze a slice and play it like an oscillator · 12 spectra + 12 presets

one tap or pulse = start a new slice now · several = set the rate
note

slice clock out
a trigger at the start of every slice
